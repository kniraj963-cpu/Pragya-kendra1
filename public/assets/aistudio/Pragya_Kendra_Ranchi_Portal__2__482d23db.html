Frequently Asked Questions

1. What documents are needed for Land Mutation (Dakhil-Kharij) in Ranchi?

Registered Sale/Gift Deed copy, Seller's previous Lagan Rasid, Khatiyan / Register-II copy, Mutation Affidavit, and Buyer's Identity Proof. You can upload all PDFs together in our Multi-PDF Portal above.

2. How long does JharSewa Caste, Income, or Residential Certificate take?

Standard Circle Officer (CO) certificates take 7 to 15 working days depending on Revenue Karamchari / CI verification. Attach clear supporting PDFs to avoid rejections.

3. Do you conduct Aadhaar enrollment or biometric updates?

No. Pragya Kendra at Radium Road is dedicated exclusively to Land Mutation, Registry Deed Drafting, Anchal Services, JharSewa Certificates, DTO, PAN, Ration Card, GST & Govt Portal applications.
